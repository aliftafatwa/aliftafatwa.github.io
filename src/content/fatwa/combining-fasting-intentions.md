## Question
Is it permissible to combine the intention of making up missed days of Ramadan with the six voluntary days of Shawwal in a single fast?

## Answer
Praise be to Allah.

According to the majority of jurists, combining an **obligatory fast** (such as making up a Ramadan day) with a **specific voluntary fast** (such as the six days of Shawwal) under a single intention does not fulfill the requirement for the voluntary fast.

1. **Obligatory Fasting:** Requires a distinct and specific intention (*Niyyah*) formed before dawn.
2. **Voluntary Fasting:** Should be performed independently to obtain the specific reward mentioned in the Sunnah.

Therefore, it is recommended to fast the missed Ramadan days first, followed by the six days of Shawwal separately.
